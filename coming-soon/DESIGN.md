# Chronicle coming-soon surface

The existing prototype establishes charcoal surfaces, warm off-white lettering and a pale green accent. The holding page preserves that palette in a quiet title-card composition for filmmakers looking at a screen in a dim editing room.

- Ground: #171918. Primary text: #e8eae1. Secondary text: #a2aca0. Accent: #d5e7ae. Decorative framing: #465043.
- Display: locally hosted Bodoni Moda, weight 500. Body: locally hosted Manrope, weights 400 and 500. Display tracking -0.04em; maximum display size 96px.
- Composition: a restrained masthead, a centered lowercase Chronicle wordmark and two-line product promise within four viewfinder corners, and a clear Coming soon footer. The decorative corners are hidden from assistive technology.
- Motion: a single 1.7-second viewfinder reveal. Text is visible by default. Reduced-motion preferences remove the animation.
- Mobile: 24px side padding, responsive wordmark, stacked footer, no horizontal overflow. There are no forms, fake controls, third-party requests or analytics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a film's opening title card, carrying only the product identity and forthcoming status.
OWN-WORLD: inherit the prototype's charcoal, off-white and pale green; crisp viewfinder geometry instead of imagery or app mockups.
STORY: visitors recognize Chronicle as an AI-native filmmaking workspace and understand it is coming soon.
FIRST VIEWPORT: quiet masthead, centered wordmark and first-idea-to-final-frame promise, status anchored at the bottom.
FORM: small static holding page inside the existing visual world; seed: none (pinned), user requested a simple coming-soon page for the existing Chronicle project.
FINISH: verify desktop and mobile renders and production DNS, HTTPS, redirects and renewal configuration.
